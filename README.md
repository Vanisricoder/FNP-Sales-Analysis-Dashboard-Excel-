# FNP-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales analysis dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to explore sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product trends, and revenue distribution across different occasions and cities.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visualizations, enabling stakeholders to identify key revenue drivers, customer spending patterns, and business growth opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance and revenue generation.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Explore revenue trends across occasions and seasons.
* Understand customer spending behavior.
* Track order and delivery performance.
* Analyze city-wise sales distribution.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain occasions such as anniversaries, festivals, and special celebrations generate significantly higher revenue.
* Product categories contribute differently to overall sales performance, helping identify high-demand segments.
* Revenue patterns vary throughout the day, highlighting peak ordering hours.
* Seasonal fluctuations influence monthly sales trends.
* A small number of products contribute a substantial share of total revenue.
* Major cities account for a large percentage of total orders.
